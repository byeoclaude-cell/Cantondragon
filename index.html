Our Story

A Scottsdale heritage,
wok-fired to order.

Since 2004, Canton Dragon has been a family-owned staple here in Scottsdale—a place built on the idea that great food tastes best in a room where you instantly feel at home.

Our kitchen is guided by our head chef, who is deeply rooted in the art of traditional Cantonese cooking. If you ask him, he’ll tell you his secret is simple: step back and let the ingredients do the talking. He obsesses over the quality of our meats and the freshness of our produce, cutting everything by hand and building every single sauce entirely from scratch. You won’t find processed shortcuts or heavy fillers here—just clean, vibrant flavors.

He takes Chinese classics you grew up loving and makes them the way they were always meant to be made. Having spent decades perfecting his craft behind the line, watching him command the roaring heat of the wok is pure poetry. Every single plate is cooked to order under his watchful eye, whether you’re craving the familiar comfort of sizzling seafood noodle and crisp General Tso’s, or looking to try one of his personal specialties, like our Mongolian beef and slow-roasted duck.

Whether you’re gathering around a big table with family or just pulling up to the bar for a happy hour cocktail, warm sake, or a glass of wine, we’re glad you’re here. Welcome to the family.
