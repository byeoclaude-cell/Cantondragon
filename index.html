Our Story

A Scottsdale heritage,
wok-fired to order.

Since 2004, Canton Dragon has been a family-owned fixture in Scottsdale, built on a simple belief: exceptional Cantonese cuisine is best enjoyed in a place that feels like home.

At the heart of our kitchen is a chef devoted to the traditions of Cantonese cooking. His philosophy is understated yet uncompromising—let remarkable ingredients speak for themselves. Premium meats, market-fresh produce, hand-cut vegetables, and sauces crafted from scratch come together without shortcuts, delivering flavors that are vibrant, balanced, and authentic.

With decades spent mastering the wok, he brings classic Chinese favorites to life with precision and artistry, while signature dishes such as our Mongolian Beef and slow-roasted duck showcase the depth of his craft. Every plate is prepared to order with meticulous care.

Whether you’re celebrating around the table with family or unwinding at the bar with a handcrafted cocktail, warm sake, or a glass of wine, we’re honored to welcome you. From our family to yours, welcome to Canton Dragon.
