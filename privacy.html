Privacy Policy

Effective July 1, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Canton Dragon Asian Grill & Bar ("Canton Dragon," "we," "us") handles information on cantondragonscottsdale.com. It covers this website only — it does not cover the practices of third-party services we link to, such as our online ordering platform or social media pages.

Information We Collect

This site has no account sign-up, contact form, or newsletter, so we do not collect or store personal information directly through it. We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate, anonymized traffic patterns — things like which pages are viewed and general device type. This analytics service does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors across other websites.

Online Ordering

When you place an order through our "Order Online" link, you leave this website and are taken to Heartland's ordering platform, which processes your name, contact details, and payment information to fulfill your order. That information is collected and handled by Heartland under its own privacy practices, not by cantondragonscottsdale.com.

Cookies & Tracking

This site does not set cookies, run advertising trackers, or sell or share visitor data. The map in our Visit section is rendered with OpenStreetMap tile imagery, which does not require a Google account and does not carry the tracking associated with an embedded Google Map.

Third-Party Links

Links to our Facebook, Instagram, and Yelp pages take you to services operated by other companies, each governed by its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review those policies before sharing information on those platforms.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally to reflect changes to the site or the services it links to. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
